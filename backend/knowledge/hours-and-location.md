## Opening hours
Bindu AI Clinic is open Monday to Saturday. Morning sessions run from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM and evening sessions from 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Individual doctors work on specific days; check the doctor's page for their schedule.

## Address and directions
The clinic is located at 12 Example Road, Sample Nagar, Hyderabad, Telangana 500001. It is a five-minute walk from the Sample Nagar bus stop. The reception desk is on the ground floor.

## Parking
Free parking for two-wheelers and cars is available behind the building. Accessible parking is next to the main entrance.
