## How to book
You can book an appointment online without creating an account. Choose a doctor, pick an available date and time, and enter your name and mobile number. You receive an appointment reference code after booking.

## Booking window
Appointments can be booked up to 30 days ahead and at least 60 minutes before the start time.

## Cancellation and rescheduling
Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before your appointment so that the slot can be offered to another patient. Online cancellation will be available after mobile verification.

## Fees and payment
The consultation fee is shown on each doctor's page. Payment is made at the clinic reception after the consultation. Cash, UPI and cards are accepted.

## Arrival
Please arrive 10 minutes early and carry any previous medical reports and a photo ID.
