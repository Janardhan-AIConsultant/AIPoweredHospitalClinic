## Is my personal information safe?
Your name, mobile number and email are encrypted before they are stored. The chat assistant never sees your personal details; you enter them in a separate form that goes directly to the clinic.

## WhatsApp messages
If you agree, the clinic sends your appointment confirmation and reminders on WhatsApp. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

## Does the assistant give medical advice?
No. The assistant only helps with finding doctors, checking times and clinic information. It cannot diagnose conditions or suggest medicines. In an emergency call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

## Which languages are supported?
The website and assistant currently work in English.

## Do I need to create an account?
No. Guest booking needs only your name and mobile number.
